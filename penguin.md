# Penguin
![paul-carroll-Y-nyDv3TWm0-unsplash](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dabb4ffe-82f3-4772-8b50-09f71afb3800)

Penguins are flightless birds that are highly adapted for life in the water. They are mostly found in the Southern Hemisphere, with a large population in Antarctica. Penguins are excellent swimmers, using their wings as flippers to propel themselves underwater.
